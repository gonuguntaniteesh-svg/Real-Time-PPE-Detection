Real-Time PPE Detection Architecture & Technical Deep-Dive

This system fine-tunes a lightweight YOLOv8-nano (3.2 Million parameters) neural network using Transfer Learning to reliably detect Personal Protective Equipment (Hard Hats, Safety Vests, Safety Goggles, and Workers) in real-time edge video feeds.

1. Core Concept Formulations

Bounding Box Representations

• Pixel Box: [x1, y1, x2, y2]
• YOLO Normalized: [cx, cy, w, h] where coordinates are divided by image dimensions (W, H).
• Non-Maximum Suppression (NMS): Sorts candidate detections by confidence, iteratively eliminating lower-scoring predictions whose IoU exceeds the 0.50 threshold.

Anchor-Free Detection

Traditional detectors (YOLOv3/v4/v5) required preset anchor boxes clustered via K-means. YOLOv8 uses an Anchor-Free Decoupled Head with TaskAlignedAssigner, regressing continuous distances [left, top, right, bottom] directly from grid cell centers.

Transfer Learning Strategy

1. Backbone Freezing: Freeze Stem & C2f layers 0-9 during the first 3 warmup epochs.
2. Adaptation: Fine-tune the PAN-FPN neck and decoupled detection head on PPE classes.
3. Cosine Annealing: Decay learning rate gradually from 0.001 to 0.00001.
